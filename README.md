## Board Summary

During H1 2026, Duka Electronics achieved stable overall sales growth driven primarily by high smartphone transaction volumes across physical stores. However, revenue growth plateaued during Q2, and net profit margins compressed due to over-reliance on low-margin hardware categories. 

While smartphones generated the highest raw revenue, accessories contributed disproportionately to overall profitability due to high gross margins (45%). Furthermore, online channel sales demonstrated strong capital efficiency compared to stagnating brick-and-mortar storefronts. 

To maximize enterprise value in H2 2026, executive management recommends shifting promotional budgets away from discounted smartphone hardware and toward high-margin accessories while expanding digital channel fulfillment capabilities.

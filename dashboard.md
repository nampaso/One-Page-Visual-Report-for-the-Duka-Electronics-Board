# Interactive Looker Studio Dashboard

## Public View Link
[Click Here to Access the Duka Electronics Sales Dashboard](https://lookerstudio.google.com/reporting/sample-duka-report)

> **Note:** Ensure your Looker Studio sharing setting is configured to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.

## Dashboard Overview & Features
The interactive dashboard provides board members and regional managers with real-time operational metrics across H1 2026:
* **3 KPI Scorecards:** Total Revenue, Total Profit, and Total Units Sold.
* **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Interactive line chart tracking performance.
* **Breakdowns:** Sales by Product Category and Sales Channel performance.
* **Controls:** Integrated Date-Range Picker and Category Dropdown Filter.
